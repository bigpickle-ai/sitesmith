What changes on day one

Threads open whole

The aggregator fetches replies from the relays where they live, not just your own. No more “Note not found” in the middle of a conversation. One connection, all the notes.

People find you by name

Your own NIP-05 (you@nostr.land) that stops impersonators and turns your profile into an address you can share. Verifiable from any client.

Stop burning mobile data

Spam filtering and data saving cut what reaches your phone. Fewer relays, less noise, less battery. Built for mobile data.

Signing up takes a minute

  1. Connect your account with the extension or your key. No email needed.
  2. Pick your name and pay from your wallet. No card, no bank details.
  3. Keep following as before: your follows and lists stay where they are.

Questions people ask before paying

Can I go back to my old relay? Yes. This is one more relay: your keys and notes are not locked in here.

What if I stop paying? You stop using the relay; your Nostr account is still yours.

Can I pay for a single month? Right now signup is 60,000 sats a year. A 5,000 sats monthly plan would make it easier for people who want to try it first.

Why trust it? The software is open and accounts can be checked on Nostr. There is no fine print to hunt for.
